A little about me:

My name is Intesar Siraj and I am on the road to become a software developer.
I was born in Saudi Arabia, grew up in Eritrea, worked with several foreign Embassies and International Organizations. I moved to the USA four years ago. I am fascinated by how technology is changing the world. I enjoy creating things and the process of building. I would like to establish a solid career path with good earning potential and career growth.
I am a detail-oriented individual who values resilience, empathy, and purpose. I also believe in speaking out against the Eritrean government and advocating for a future where people can live without fear, with freedom of speech, and with equal rights. My experiences have shaped my commitment to justice, human dignity, and the belief that everyone deserves to live with safety and opportunity. I am especially driven by the hope of one day seeing my friends and family free, and seeing Eritrea become a place where people are no longer silenced or oppressed.
